# Rule: Ponytail Design - Institutional Elegance & Zero AI Slop

## Design Directives
1. **Institutional Modernism**: Clean European municipal data aesthetic.
   - Background: Canvas Light `#F8FAFC`, Surface `#FFFFFF`.
   - Primary: Civic Teal `#0D9488`, Dark Teal `#0F766E`, Sky Blue `#0284C7`.
   - Alert Accents: Critical Red `#E11D48`, Warning Orange `#EA580C`, Success Emerald `#059669`.
   - Borders: Hairline `#E2E8F0` and `#CBD5E1`.
   - Shadows: Diffuse hairline `0 1px 3px rgba(15,23,42,0.04)`.
2. **Typography Standards**:
   - Plus Jakarta Sans for titles and headers.
   - Inter with OpenType tabular digits (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) for all stats, coordinates, and countdown clocks.
   - Full Urdu localization with Noto Nastaliq line-height spacing.
3. **Components & Layout**:
   - Match the 15 screens from CivicCare institutional master screenshot pixel-for-pixel.
   - All interactive controls (pills, badges, sliders, steppers, maps, charts) must have fluid hover states and active indicators.
